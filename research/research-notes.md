# Perennial Bed Planner – research notes

Kept so the research does not have to be redone. Checked October 2026.

## Good neighbors (companion plantings)

Each entry: the plant the source is about, what the source says, the partners in our plant list, and the source link.

- **Virginia Bluebells** – Hostas and ferns both work well as companions; use other plants to hide gaps after it goes dormant.  
  Partners in the planner: Hosta, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://www.perennials.com/plants/mertensia-virginica.html
- **Brunnera 'Jack Frost'** – Contrasts nicely with Japanese painted fern; combines well with astilbes, bleeding heart and Heuchera.  
  Partners in the planner: Japanese Painted Fern, Astilbe, Bleeding Heart, Coral Bells  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/brunnera-macrophylla-jack-frost
- **Wild Blue Phlox** – Goes well with Jack-in-the-pulpit; combine with hosta, astilbe, bleeding heart, columbine, hellebore, heuchera, lungwort and ferns.  
  Partners in the planner: Jack-in-the-Pulpit, Hosta, Astilbe, Bleeding Heart, Wild Columbine, Lenten Rose, Coral Bells, Prairie Alumroot, Lungwort, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/woodland-phlox-phlox-divaricata/
- **Maidenhair Fern** – An excellent addition to hosta gardens.  
  Partners in the planner: Hosta  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/northern-maidenhair-fern-adiantum-pedatum/
- **Wild Geranium** – Found with Solomon's seal, ferns, mayapple and woodland phlox; combines with celandine poppy, columbine, foamflower and goat's beard.  
  Partners in the planner: Solomon's Seal, Mayapple, Wild Blue Phlox, Celandine Poppy, Wild Columbine, Foamflower, Goat's Beard, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/wild-geranium-geranium-maculatum/
- **White Turtlehead** – Combines well with Shasta daisy 'Becky'.  
  Partners in the planner: Shasta Daisy  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/turtlehead-chelone-spp/
- **Goat's Beard** – Combines well with astilbe, columbine and ferns; plant with wild ginger, Virginia bluebells, Jacob's ladder and wild geranium; mix with iris.  
  Partners in the planner: Astilbe, Wild Columbine, Wild Ginger, Virginia Bluebells, Jacob's Ladder, Wild Geranium, Siberian Iris, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/goatsbeard-aruncus-dioicus/
- **Virginia Waterleaf** – Commonly found with wild columbine, wild ginger, wild geranium, woodland phlox and mayapple.  
  Partners in the planner: Wild Columbine, Wild Ginger, Wild Geranium, Wild Blue Phlox, Mayapple  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/virginia-waterleaf-hydrophyllum-virginianum/
- **Blazing Star** – Combines with yellow flowers such as coreopsis and goldenrod, pink ones such as purple coneflower, prairie grasses and silver plants such as Stachys (lamb's ear).  
  Partners in the planner: Lanceleaf Coreopsis, Purple Coneflower, Lamb's Ear, Little Bluestem, Prairie Dropseed  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/liatris/
- **Anise Hyssop** – Combines well with purple coneflower, bee balm and native grasses; in gardens with Japanese anemones.  
  Partners in the planner: Purple Coneflower, Wild Bergamot, Japanese Anemone, Little Bluestem, Prairie Dropseed  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/anise-hyssop-agastache-foeniculum/
- **Sneezeweed** – Combines well with ornamental grasses, anise hyssop, beebalm, daylilies, fall asters, liatris, purple coneflower and rudbeckias.  
  Partners in the planner: Anise Hyssop, Wild Bergamot, Daylily, New England Aster, Purple Coneflower, Blazing Star, Black-eyed Susan, Little Bluestem, Prairie Dropseed  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/helenium-helenium-autumnale/
- **Solomon's Seal** – Combines well with bleeding hearts, ferns, lungworts, bergenia, wild ginger, astilbes and hostas.  
  Partners in the planner: Bleeding Heart, Lungwort, Bergenia, Wild Ginger, Astilbe, Hosta, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/solomons-seal-polygonatum-spp/
- **Bleeding Heart** – Pairs with Virginia bluebells in spring; ferns, hosta, hardy geranium and astilbe fill in as it dies back.  
  Partners in the planner: Virginia Bluebells, Hosta, Geranium 'Rozanne', Bigroot Geranium, Astilbe, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/bleeding-heart-dicentra-spectabilis/
- **Lady's Mantle** – Contrasts nicely with vertical perennials such as iris, liatris, ferns and salvia.  
  Partners in the planner: Bearded Iris, Siberian Iris, Blazing Star, Salvia 'May Night', Salvia 'Caradonna', Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/ladys-mantle-alchemilla-mollis/
- **Masterwort** – Combine with astilbe, ligularia and ferns in shade; fine foliage contrasts with hostas.  
  Partners in the planner: Astilbe, Ligularia 'The Rocket', Hosta, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/astrantia-astrantia-major/
- **Japanese Forest Grass** – Combines well with hosta and ferns; eye-catching near dark-leaved Heuchera, Pulmonaria or Brunnera; a backdrop for dark astilbe.  
  Partners in the planner: Hosta, Coral Bells, Lungwort, Brunnera 'Jack Frost', Astilbe, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/japanese-forest-grass-hakonechloa-macra/
- **Yellow Foxglove** – Combines with catmint and lady's mantle; in shade with astilbe, hosta or coral bells.  
  Partners in the planner: Catmint 'Walker's Low', Lady's Mantle, Astilbe, Hosta, Coral Bells  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/yellow-foxglove-digitalis-grandiflora/
- **Lenten Rose** – Combines with barrenwort in spring; later a backdrop for hostas; shown among hardy geranium.  
  Partners in the planner: Barrenwort, Hosta, Geranium 'Rozanne', Bigroot Geranium  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/lenten-rose-helleborus-xhybridus/
- **Toad Lily** – Combines well with anemone, astilbe, ferns, hellebores, hosta and lungwort.  
  Partners in the planner: Japanese Anemone, Astilbe, Lenten Rose, Hosta, Lungwort, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/hairy-toad-lily-tricyrtis-hirta/
- **Virginia Bluebells** – Combines with bleeding heart and celandine poppy; hosta, astilbe, bugbane, Solomon's seal or ferns fill the gap after it goes dormant.  
  Partners in the planner: Bleeding Heart, Celandine Poppy, Hosta, Astilbe, Black Cohosh, Solomon's Seal, Maidenhair Fern, Ostrich Fern, Christmas Fern, Lady Fern, Cinnamon Fern  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/virginia-bluebells-mertensia-virginica/
- **Japanese Anemone** – Pair with fall aster, bugbane, eupatorium, goldenrod, sedum or toad lily; near bleeding heart; with variegated hosta and grasses.  
  Partners in the planner: New England Aster, Heart-leaved Aster, Black Cohosh, Sweet Joe-Pye Weed, Sedum 'Autumn Joy', Toad Lily, Bleeding Heart, Hosta, Little Bluestem, Prairie Dropseed  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/anemone-honorine-jobert/
- **Wild Columbine** – Combines well with hostas and astilbe in shade gardens, and with wild geranium and wild ginger in natural settings.  
  Partners in the planner: Hosta, Astilbe, Wild Geranium, Wild Ginger  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/eastern-red-columbine-aquilegia-canadensis/
- **Cardinal Flower** – Combines well with great blue lobelia, irises and marsh milkweed; also found with purple coneflower, blazing star and foxglove beardtongue.  
  Partners in the planner: Great Blue Lobelia, Siberian Iris, Swamp Milkweed, Purple Coneflower, Blazing Star, Foxglove Beardtongue  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/cardinal-flower-lobelia-cardinalis/
- **Butterfly Weed** – Combine with Penstemon 'Husker Red' or purple speedwells; works well with coreopsis, purple coneflower, blazing star, black-eyed Susan, little bluestem and prairie dropseed.  
  Partners in the planner: Foxglove Beardtongue, Veronica 'Royal Candles', Lanceleaf Coreopsis, Purple Coneflower, Blazing Star, Black-eyed Susan, Little Bluestem, Prairie Dropseed  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/butterflyweed-asclepias-tuberosa/
- **Prairie Dropseed** – Combined with fall-blooming sedums; a great filler between purple coneflower, liatris, black-eyed Susan and butterfly weed.  
  Partners in the planner: Sedum 'Autumn Joy', Purple Coneflower, Blazing Star, Black-eyed Susan, Butterfly Weed, Little Bluestem, Lanceleaf Coreopsis  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/prairie-dropseed-sporobolus-heterolepis/
- **Little Bluestem** – Pair with dark-leaved Heuchera; combine with aster, tall sedums like 'Autumn Joy', yarrow, rudbeckias and purple coneflower.  
  Partners in the planner: Coral Bells, Prairie Alumroot, New England Aster, Sedum 'Autumn Joy', Yarrow 'Moonshine', Black-eyed Susan, Purple Coneflower  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/little-bluestem-schizachyrium-scoparium/
- **Catmint 'Walker's Low'** – Combines well with beebalm and Shasta daisies; harmonizes with yellow foxglove, Coreopsis and lemon daylilies.  
  Partners in the planner: Wild Bergamot, Shasta Daisy, Yellow Foxglove, Lanceleaf Coreopsis, Daylily  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/nepeta-x-faassenii-walkers-low/
- **Foxglove Beardtongue** – Combines well with blue spiderworts, blue false indigo and prairie dropseed; try it with catmint and lady's mantle.  
  Partners in the planner: Spiderwort, Wild Blue Indigo, Prairie Dropseed, Catmint 'Walker's Low', Lady's Mantle  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/husker-red-foxglove-beardtongue-penstemon-digitalis-husker-red/
- **Prairie Smoke** – Grows well with blazing star and little bluestem; often grows with prairie dropseed in natural settings.  
  Partners in the planner: Blazing Star, Little Bluestem, Prairie Dropseed  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/prairie-smoke-geum-triflorum/
- **Rattlesnake Master** – Combines well with other prairie plants, such as Liatris and little bluestem.  
  Partners in the planner: Blazing Star, Little Bluestem  
  Source: https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/rattlesnake-master-eryngium-yuccifolium/
- **Pennsylvania Sedge** – Heuchera 'Brownies' gives a bolder contrast to the sedge's fine leaves at a woodland edge.  
  Partners in the planner: Coral Bells  
  Source: https://mtcubacenter.org/plants/pennsylvania-sedge/
- **Obedient Plant** – A border is brightened when obedient plant is planted alongside goldenrods, little bluestem and purple coneflower.  
  Partners in the planner: Little Bluestem, Purple Coneflower  
  Source: https://mtcubacenter.org/plants/obedient-plant/
- **Golden Alexanders** – Plant at the edge of a meadow with butterfly weed and New England aster.  
  Partners in the planner: Butterfly Weed, New England Aster  
  Source: https://mtcubacenter.org/plants/common-golden-alexanders/
- **Culver's Root** – Pair with purple coneflower for an interplay of flower and foliage.  
  Partners in the planner: Purple Coneflower  
  Source: https://mtcubacenter.org/plants/culvers-root/
- **Salvia 'Caradonna'** – Walters Gardens (Zeeland, Michigan) lists varieties of these as companions: Geranium 'Rozanne', Shasta daisy 'Becky' and garden phlox.  
  Partners in the planner: Geranium 'Rozanne', Shasta Daisy, Garden Phlox
