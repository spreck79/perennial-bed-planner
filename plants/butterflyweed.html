Butterfly Weed

Asclepias tuberosa
Michigan native! Toxic to people, dogs and cats

A monarch host plant with blazing orange flowers. Deep taproot, so plant it once and don't move it. Late to emerge in spring.

  • Spring prep Late May: comes up late, mark the spot
    Slow to come up, often not until late May. Mark the spot so it isn't dug up or planted over by mistake.
  • Cut back Mid-Apr to early May: cut old stems
    Leave the stems standing over winter and cut them back in spring, before the new shoots appear.
  • Divide Don't divide
    Don't divide: the deep taproot breaks and the plant rarely recovers.
