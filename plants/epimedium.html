Barrenwort

Epimedium, garden hybrids
◆ Garden favorite

A tough, low plant with heart-shaped leaves that thrives under trees. Dainty spring flowers, and slow to spread.

At a glance

Height8–12 in
Spacing15 in apart (center to center)
Place in the bedFront row
BloomsApr – May
ColorDainty yellow, pink or white
LightPart shade to shade; handles dry shade once established
Grows in shade, or with up to 6 hours of direct sun a day
Soil moistureDry to average
Plant typeLow, spreading groundcover
Hardiness zones5a to 9b (hardy in Michigan zone 6B)
DeerRarely damaged (Rutgers rating A)
ToxicBelieved non-toxic to people, dogs and cats
Code on the planBarr

Bloom
Cut back
  • Cut back Early Apr: cut old leaves to the ground
    No fall cutting. Cut the old leaves to the ground in early April, before the flowers appear, so the blooms aren't hidden.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep
    Divide in late August to September, if the clump is crowded or flowers less. Allow 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes.

Goes well with

  • Lenten Rose
    Combines with barrenwort in spring; later a backdrop for hostas; shown among hardy geranium. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Lenten Rose article
