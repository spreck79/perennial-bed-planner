Mayapple

Podophyllum peltatum
Michigan native! Toxic to people, dogs and cats

Umbrella-like leaves that carpet the woodland floor in spring, then die back by midsummer. Spreads into colonies.

  • Spring prep Early Apr: mark the spot, don't rake
    Comes up very early: mark the spot and don't rake or mulch over the shoots.
  • Cut back Jun to early Jul: clear leaves once yellow
    Goes dormant in summer. Let the leaves yellow and fade on their own (they feed the roots), then clear them away. Nearby ferns or hostas fill the gap.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep
    Divide in late August to September, if the clump is crowded or flowers less. Allow 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes.

Goes well with

  • Virginia Waterleaf
    Commonly found with wild columbine, wild ginger, wild geranium, woodland phlox and mayapple. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Virginia Waterleaf article
  • Wild Geranium
    Found with Solomon's seal, ferns, mayapple and woodland phlox; combines with celandine poppy, columbine, foamflower and goat's beard. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Wild Geranium article
