Bigroot Geranium

Geranium macrorrhizum
◆ Garden favorite

A fragrant, spreading groundcover that crowds out weeds, with leaves that turn red in fall. Handles dry shade well.

  • Cut back Early Apr: snip winter-browned leaves
    Evergreen or semi-evergreen: no fall cutting. In early spring, snip off leaves that winter browned.
  • Optional trim Optional: shear after first flowers
    Optional: once the first flowers fade, shear the old stems back to the new leaves at the base. Many rebloom in 5 to 6 weeks.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep
    Divide in late August to September, if the clump is crowded or flowers less. Allow 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes.

Goes well with

  • Bleeding Heart
    Pairs with Virginia bluebells in spring; ferns, hosta, hardy geranium and astilbe fill in as it dies back. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Bleeding Heart article
  • Lenten Rose
    Combines with barrenwort in spring; later a backdrop for hostas; shown among hardy geranium. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Lenten Rose article
