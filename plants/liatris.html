Blazing Star

Liatris spicata
Michigan native✿ Daisy family

Tall purple spikes that open from the top down, loved by bees and butterflies. Grows from a corm and stays narrow. Daisy family, like ragweed.

Spring prep
Cut back
  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back Mid-Apr to mid-May: cut to 8 to 24 in
    Spring cleanup, once days stay around 50°F (mid-April to mid-May): cut last year's stems down to 8 to 24 in. New growth hides the stubble within weeks, and native bees nest in the hollow stems.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as the shoots come up, if the clump is crowded or the middle dies out.

Goes well with

  • Lamb's EarLanceleaf CoreopsisLittle BluestemPurple Coneflower
    Combines with yellow flowers such as coreopsis and goldenrod, pink ones such as purple coneflower, prairie grasses and silver plants such as Stachys (lamb's ear). Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Blazing Star article
  • Prairie Dropseed
    Combines with yellow flowers such as coreopsis and goldenrod, pink ones such as purple coneflower, prairie grasses and silver plants such as Stachys (lamb's ear). Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Blazing Star article, Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Prairie Dropseed article
  • Butterfly Weed
    Combine with Penstemon 'Husker Red' or purple speedwells; works well with coreopsis, purple coneflower, blazing star, black-eyed Susan, little bluestem and prairie dropseed. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Butterfly Weed article
  • Cardinal Flower
    Combines well with great blue lobelia, irises and marsh milkweed; also found with purple coneflower, blazing star and foxglove beardtongue. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Cardinal Flower article
  • Lady's Mantle
    Contrasts nicely with vertical perennials such as iris, liatris, ferns and salvia. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Lady's Mantle article
  • Prairie Smoke
    Grows well with blazing star and little bluestem; often grows with prairie dropseed in natural settings. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Prairie Smoke article
  • Rattlesnake Master
    Combines well with other prairie plants, such as Liatris and little bluestem. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Rattlesnake Master article
  • Sea Holly
    Walters Gardens (Zeeland, Michigan) lists varieties of these as companions: Liatris and Coreopsis. Walters Gardens, Zeeland, Michigan: Sea Holly companions
  • Sneezeweed
    Combines well with ornamental grasses, anise hyssop, beebalm, daylilies, fall asters, liatris, purple coneflower and rudbeckias. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Sneezeweed article
