Foxglove Beardtongue

Penstemon digitalis
Michigan native Deer sometimes eat it

Clouds of white tubular flowers in early summer over glossy leaves. A favorite of bumblebees.

Spring prep
Cut back
  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back Mid-Apr to mid-May: cut to 8 to 24 in
    Spring cleanup, once days stay around 50°F (mid-April to mid-May): cut last year's stems down to 8 to 24 in. New growth hides the stubble within weeks, and native bees nest in the hollow stems.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep
    Divide in late August to September, if the clump is crowded or flowers less. Allow 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes.

Goes well with

  • Catmint 'Walker's Low'Lady's MantlePrairie DropseedSpiderwortWild Blue Indigo
    Combines well with blue spiderworts, blue false indigo and prairie dropseed; try it with catmint and lady's mantle. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Foxglove Beardtongue article
  • Butterfly Weed
    Combine with Penstemon 'Husker Red' or purple speedwells; works well with coreopsis, purple coneflower, blazing star, black-eyed Susan, little bluestem and prairie dropseed. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Butterfly Weed article
  • Cardinal Flower
    Combines well with great blue lobelia, irises and marsh milkweed; also found with purple coneflower, blazing star and foxglove beardtongue. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Cardinal Flower article
  • Garden Phlox
    Walters Gardens (Zeeland, Michigan) lists varieties of these as companions: purple coneflower and Penstemon. Walters Gardens, Zeeland, Michigan: Garden Phlox companions
