Morrow's Sedge

Carex morrowii
◆ Garden favorite

An evergreen sedge that makes a neat, dark-green fountain of blades all year. Clumping and low-maintenance.

At a glance

Height10–16 in
Spacing15 in apart (center to center)
Place in the bedFront row
BloomsGrown for its leaves
ColorDark green, often white-edged blades
LightPart shade to shade; average to moist soil
Grows in shade, or with up to 6 hours of direct sun a day
Soil moistureAverage to moist
Plant typeGrass or sedge
Hardiness zones5a to 9b (hardy in Michigan zone 6B)
DeerRarely damaged (Rutgers rating A)
ToxicBelieved non-toxic to people, dogs and cats
Code on the planMoSe

Through the year

AprMayJunJulAugSepOctNov
Cut back
  • Cut back Early Apr: snip brown blades
    Evergreen: no fall cutting. In early April, snip off any brown or tattered blades.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as new growth starts, if the clump gets too big.
