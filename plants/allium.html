Allium 'Purple Sensation'

Allium aflatunense 'Purple Sensation'
◆ Garden favorite! Toxic to dogs and cats

Baseball-sized purple globes on tall stems in late spring. Plant 6 to 8 inches deep in October among perennials that hide the leaves as they fade. Deer, rabbits and squirrels leave it alone.

  • Spring prep Oct: plant 6 to 8 in deep
    Plant new bulbs in October, 6 to 8 in deep in clumps, before the ground freezes.
  • Cut back 6+ weeks after leaves fade: cut off
    Leave the leaves alone for at least 6 weeks after they start to fade (they feed next year's flowers), then cut them off.
  • Divide After leaves die, if crowded
    If a clump gets crowded and flowers less, dig and split it after the leaves die back.
