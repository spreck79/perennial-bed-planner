Veronica 'Royal Candles'

Veronica spicata 'Royal Candles'
◆ Garden favorite

Compact spikes of deep blue all summer if you snip off the spent ones. A neat front-of-bed plant.

At a glance

Height12–18 in
Spacing15 in apart (center to center)
Place in the bedMiddle row
BloomsJun – Aug
ColorDeep blue spikes
LightFull sun
Needs 6 or more hours of direct sun a day

Spring prep
Cut back
  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back Late Oct to Nov, after hard frosts (blackened leaves)
    Cut back in fall, after a few hard frosts (usually late October to November): the leaves blacken and add nothing to the winter garden.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as the shoots come up, if the clump is crowded or the middle dies out.

Goes well with

  • Butterfly Weed
    Combine with Penstemon 'Husker Red' or purple speedwells; works well with coreopsis, purple coneflower, blazing star, black-eyed Susan, little bluestem and prairie dropseed. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Butterfly Weed article
