Yarrow 'Moonshine'

Achillea 'Moonshine'
◆ Garden favorite✿ Daisy family! Toxic to dogs and cats

Flat lemon-yellow flower heads over ferny silver leaves. Drought tolerant and long blooming. Daisy family, like ragweed.

Spring prep
Cut back
  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back Late Oct to Nov, after hard frosts (keep low base leaves)
    Cut back in fall, after a few hard frosts (usually late October to November): cut the flower stalks but leave the low new leaves at the base.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as the shoots come up, if the clump is crowded or the middle dies out.

Goes well with

  • English Lavender
    Walters Gardens (Zeeland, Michigan) lists varieties of these as companions: yarrow, Shasta daisy, catmint 'Walker's Low' and Salvia nemorosa. Walters Gardens, Zeeland, Michigan: English Lavender companions
  • Little Bluestem
    Pair with dark-leaved Heuchera; combine with aster, tall sedums like 'Autumn Joy', yarrow, rudbeckias and purple coneflower. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Little Bluestem article
  • Salvia 'May Night'
    Walters Gardens (Zeeland, Michigan) lists varieties of these as companions: yarrow and Shasta daisy 'Becky'. Walters Gardens, Zeeland, Michigan: Salvia 'May Night' companions
  • Shasta Daisy
    Walters Gardens (Zeeland, Michigan) lists varieties of these as companions: yarrow, daylily, iris, catmint and sedum. Walters Gardens, Zeeland, Michigan: Shasta Daisy companions
