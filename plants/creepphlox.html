Creeping Phlox

Phlox subulata
◆ Garden favorite Deer sometimes eat it

A low evergreen mat that smothers itself in flowers in spring. Great at the front edge or spilling over a wall.

At a glance

Height4–6 in
Spacing18 in apart (center to center)
Place in the bedFront row
BloomsApr – May
ColorPink, lavender or white carpet
LightFull sun
Needs 6 or more hours of direct sun a day
Soil moistureDry to average
Plant typeLow, spreading groundcover; good for filling gaps
Hardiness zones3a to 9b (hardy in Michigan zone 6B)
DeerOccasionally severely damaged (Rutgers rating C)
ToxicBelieved non-toxic to people, dogs and cats
Code on the planCrPh

Bloom
Cut back
  • Cut back Early Apr: snip winter-browned leaves
    Evergreen or semi-evergreen: no fall cutting. In early spring, snip off leaves that winter browned.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep
    Divide in late August to September, if the clump is crowded or flowers less. Allow 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes.

  • Bearded IrisBergenia
