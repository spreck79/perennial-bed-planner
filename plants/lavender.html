English Lavender

Lavandula angustifolia
◆ Garden favorite! Toxic to dogs and cats

Fragrant silver-gray mounds with purple spikes in summer. Needs sharp drainage; wet winter soil kills it.

At a glance

Height12–24 in
Spacing18 in apart (center to center)

BloomsJun – Jul

LightFull sun

  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back May, after new growth: trim to live buds
    Don't cut in fall. Wait until new growth is well under way in May, then cut out dead stems and trim live ones back to healthy buds.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as the shoots come up, if the clump is crowded or the middle dies out.
