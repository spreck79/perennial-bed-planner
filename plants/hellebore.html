Lenten Rose

Helleborus × hybridus
◆ Garden favorite! Toxic to people, dogs and cats

The first flowers of the year, often open in March, with evergreen leaves. Long-lived and deer resistant.

Bloom
Cut back
  • Cut back Early Apr: cut off last year's leaves
    Evergreen: no fall cutting. In early spring, as the flower buds rise, cut off last year's leaves at the base so the flowers and new leaves show.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep
    Divide in late August to September, if the clump is crowded or flowers less. Allow 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes.

Goes well with

  • BarrenwortBigroot GeraniumGeranium 'Rozanne'Hosta
    Combines with barrenwort in spring; later a backdrop for hostas; shown among hardy geranium. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Lenten Rose article
  • Toad Lily
    Combines well with anemone, astilbe, ferns, hellebores, hosta and lungwort. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Toad Lily article
  • Wild Blue Phlox
    Goes well with Jack-in-the-pulpit; combine with hosta, astilbe, bleeding heart, columbine, hellebore, heuchera, lungwort and ferns. Wisconsin Horticulture (UW–Madison Extension): Wild Blue Phlox article
