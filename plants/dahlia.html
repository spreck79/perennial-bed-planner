Dahlia

Dahlia pinnata
◆ Garden favorite✿ Daisy family Deer sometimes eat it! Toxic to people, dogs and cats❄ Not winter-hardy here

Big, showy flowers from midsummer until frost, in almost every color, and great for cutting. Not winter-hardy here: dig the tubers after the first frost and replant them in May. Daisy family, like ragweed.

    Cut the stems down to a few inches when you dig it up or bring it in.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as the shoots come up, if the clump is crowded or the middle dies out.
  • Winter dig up in fall, replant in May
    Not winter-hardy here (hardy to zone 7a). After the first frost blackens the leaves (usually mid to late October), dig it up, let it dry, and store it somewhere cool and frost-free (35 to 45°F) over winter. Replant after the last frost, usually mid to late May.
