Garden Peony

Paeonia lactiflora
◆ Garden favorite! Toxic to dogs and cats

Huge fragrant flowers in late spring and handsome leaves all season. Plant the eyes only 1-2 inches deep. Lives for decades.

  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back Late Oct to Nov, after hard frosts (leaf blight)
    Cut back in fall, after a few hard frosts (usually late October to November): the leaves blacken and can carry leaf blight into next year.
  • Divide Late Aug to Sep, if crowded
    Divide only if crowded, in late August to September (fleshy roots do best in fall). Replant peony eyes just 1½ in deep.
