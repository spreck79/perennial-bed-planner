Palm Sedge

Carex muskingumensis
Michigan native

A graceful sedge with leaves that radiate like tiny palm trees. Likes damp soil and turns gold in fall.

At a glance

Height18–36 in
Spacing18 in apart (center to center)
Place in the bedMiddle row
BloomsGrown for its leaves
ColorBright green, palm-like whorls
LightPart shade to sun; moist soil
Needs at least 3 hours of direct sun a day
Soil moistureMoist to wet
Plant typeGrass or sedge
Hardiness zones4a to 7b (hardy in Michigan zone 6B)
DeerRarely damaged (Rutgers rating A)
ToxicBelieved non-toxic to people, dogs and cats
Code on the planPaSe

Through the year

AprMayJunJulAugSepOctNov
Cut back
  • Cut back Early Apr: comb out old blades
    Leave over winter. In early April, comb out or trim the old brown blades before new growth starts.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as new growth starts, if the clump gets too big.
