Autumn Fern

Dryopteris erythrosora
◆ Garden favorite❄ Mulch for winter

New fronds unfurl copper-orange and mature to glossy green, and the plant stays evergreen in mild winters.

At a glance

Cut back
Winter mulch
  • Cut back Apr: snip ragged old fronds
    Evergreen: no fall cutting. In spring the old fronds wither as the new ones uncurl; snip them off then if they look ragged.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as new growth starts, if the clump gets too big.
  • Winter mulch the crown for winter
    Hardy only to zone 6B, the edge of what it can take. Once the ground freezes (usually late November), cover the crown with about 4 to 6 in of shredded leaves or straw, and pull it back in early April.
