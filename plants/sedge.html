Pennsylvania Sedge

Carex pensylvanica
Michigan native

A fine, soft groundcover that knits the bed together, fills gaps and crowds out weeds. Rarely needs mowing or trimming.

At a glance

Height6–10 in
Spacing10 in apart (center to center)
Place in the bedFront row
BloomsGrown for its leaves
ColorSoft green, grass-like
LightPart shade to shade; handles dry shade under trees
Grows in shade, or with up to 6 hours of direct sun a day
Soil moistureDry to average
Plant typeGrass or sedge; good for filling gaps
Hardiness zones3a to 8b (hardy in Michigan zone 6B)
DeerRarely damaged (Rutgers rating A)
ToxicBelieved non-toxic to people, dogs and cats
Code on the planPeSe

Cut back
  • Cut back Early Apr: comb out old blades
    Leave over winter. In early April, comb out or trim the old brown blades before new growth starts.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as new growth starts, if the clump gets too big.
