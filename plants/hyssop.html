Anise Hyssop

Agastache foeniculum
◆ Garden favorite

Licorice-scented leaves and lavender spikes all summer, one of the best bee plants there is. Native to the northern prairies. Short-lived but reseeds.

Spring prep
Cut back
  • Spring prep Early Apr: pull mulch off the crown
    Early April, as shoots show: pull back any winter mulch from the crown and clear soggy leaves off the new growth.
  • Cut back May: cut back (leave tops all winter)
    Leave the tops standing over winter: they catch leaves and snow that insulate the crown. Cut back in May once new growth shows.
  • Divide Spring, as shoots come up
    Divide in spring as the shoots come up, if the clump is crowded or the middle dies out.
