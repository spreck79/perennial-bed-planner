Small to big, with spacing

A side view of the chosen plants from shortest to tallest, drawn to one scale. The bar under each plant is how far apart to set that kind. Click a plant to highlight it on the bed plan and in the bloom chart; Shift- or Ctrl-click to highlight several.

Mixing two kinds side by side? Average their two spacings. Plant the big back-row plants first, then the middle, then the front, and finish by tucking the low plants into the gaps.
