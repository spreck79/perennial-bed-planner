Each plant is drawn at its full grown size, so the plan is also your spacing guide. Drag a plant to move it. Click a plant, then press Delete to remove it. Grid squares are 1 foot.

When each plant blooms

Tick a row to add or remove that plant. Hover a row to see where it sits in the bed. Striped bars are foliage plants that stay green and carry the bed between flowers. ◆ marks a garden favorite (not native). ✿ marks the daisy family (related to ragweed).

Chosen plants in bloom

Small to big, with spacing

A side view of the chosen plants from shortest to tallest, drawn to one scale. The bar under each plant is how far apart to set that kind.

Mixing two kinds side by side? Average their two spacings. Plant the big back-row plants first, then the middle, then the front, and finish by tucking the low plants into the gaps.
