Good neighbors (companion plantings)

When you select a plant on the bed plan, the options bar suggests plants that go well with it, and the PDF lists them as "Good neighbors". Every pairing comes from a published source: a university extension garden article, a public garden, or a Michigan grower's companion list. The reason shown in the planner is what that source says.
